How it works
01 — Read
Connects to your Spotify. Reads all your saved tracks and top artists across short, medium, and long-term listening windows.
02 — Analyse
Pulls audio features for every track — valence, energy, tempo, danceability, acousticness, instrumentalness. Enriches with Last.fm community tags and MusicBrainz metadata.
03 — Classify
Each track is scored against 61 mood profiles using weighted audio feature math. Confidence scores determine primary and cross-mood assignments.
04 — Arrange
Playlists are shaped by adaptive energy arcs, BPM flow optimization, and Camelot Wheel harmonic matching. Every transition is smooth. Every opener hooks.

Questions

Frequently asked

What is Point Woebegone?
Point Woebegone is a cybersecurity and privacy company that builds tools for the decentralized economy. Our products include WoeShield (threat detection) and Moodwave (music mood intelligence — audio feature analysis, Camelot Wheel, BPM flow optimization).
What is WoeShield?
WoeShield is an AI-powered threat detection tool. Paste any suspicious email, link, message, or crypto transaction and WoeShield analyses it to tell you if it is safe, suspicious, or dangerous. No account needed.
What is Moodwave?
Moodwave is a music mood intelligence tool. It reads your Spotify library, analyses every track's audio features — energy, valence, tempo, danceability, acousticness — and classifies them into 61 moods using music theory. Camelot Wheel harmonic matching, BPM flow optimization, adaptive energy arcs. $4.99 one-time. Zero data stored.
How does Point Woebegone handle privacy?
Privacy by design. Both WoeShield and Moodwave process data in real-time and never store it on our servers. No accounts required for WoeShield. No tracking. No data harvesting.
Does Point Woebegone store my data?
No. Both WoeShield and Moodwave are built with zero data storage. Your data is processed in real-time and never stored on our servers. Privacy by design.
How much does Moodwave cost?
Moodwave costs $4.99 one-time. No subscription. No recurring fees. Limited to 200 lifetime spots. Pay once and use it forever.
What is decentralized trust?
Decentralized trust means building systems where trust is verifiable, auditable, and earned rather than assumed. Point Woebegone builds tools that make this possible in the crypto and privacy space.
