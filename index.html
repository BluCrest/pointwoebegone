The name is the thesis

Woebegone is how the world feels. Point is where it ends.

Woebegone means worn down by sorrow. It is exactly how ordinary people feel about the modern internet: burnt by scams, confused by complexity, betrayed by promises.

A point is a place of change. A turning point. A coordinate where one thing ends and another begins.

Point Woebegone is that coordinate: the place the story of fear ends. AI is everywhere. Crypto is changing everything. The missing piece is trust you can check, privacy you do not have to beg for, and proof you do not need a degree to read. That is what we are here to build.
